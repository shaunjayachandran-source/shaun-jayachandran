Coaching & Hoops Creating Hope

What coaching and a nonprofit taught me about building products.

I've coached for 20+ years and founded Hoops Creating Hope to use basketball to educate youth in India. Both shape how I work: start with the people closest to the problem, then measure what changes for them.

Start with the person

Coaching starts with listening. I bring the same habit to user research.

Build with, not for

I design programs with the kids and families they serve, not for them.

Measure what changes

80+ of the athletes I've coached have gone on to college programs, and 60% of the youth in Hoops Creating Hope are girls.
