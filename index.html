About

From the classroom to the court to the product roadmap

Shaun Jayachandran has spent his career at the intersection of education, sport, and technology. Raised in Calgary, Alberta and educated in the Washington, D.C. area, Shaun began as a classroom chemistry teacher, varsity coach, and admissions leader before founding Crossover Basketball and Scholars Academy — a nonprofit that uses basketball as a vehicle to advance gender equity, critical thinking, and education access for marginalized students in India.

Today, Shaun brings that same builder's mindset to sports technology as an AI Product Advisor at GameRun.ai, helping shape AI-powered athlete-analysis tools used to evaluate performance and injury risk. He moves comfortably between a classroom, a boardroom, and a stage — always telling the same story: sport, used well, builds leaders.

Experience

Where the work has happened

  1. AI Product Advisor GameRun.ai

    Advising product for an AI-powered athlete-analysis platform used by teams, scouts, and programs to evaluate on-court performance, development trajectory, and injury risk — camera-system agnostic and built for real game footage.

  2. Founder & CEO Crossover Basketball and Scholars Academy

    Built a nonprofit that brings student-athletes and educators together around basketball to teach leadership, gender equity, and critical thinking — reaching thousands of students across marginalized communities in India and earning recognition from organizations including the Philadelphia 76ers and Indiaspora.

  3. Teacher, Coach & Admissions Leader Independent Schools

    Taught chemistry, coached varsity basketball and football, and led diversity and admissions initiatives — the foundation for a career spent building people, not just products.
