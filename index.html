Signature Talks

The Human Game.

Three conversations about what technology can do — and what we should never ask it to do for us.

01
Keynote · Parents / Educators

Raising Humans in the Age of AI

For parents, educators & schools When AI can do the work, what should we still teach kids to do?

How do we raise young people who can use AI without outsourcing their judgment, effort, relationships or responsibility? Shaun brings 20+ years of coaching and youth development together with his experience building technology to give parents and educators a practical framework for raising thoughtful humans in an AI-saturated world.

02
Keynote · Leaders / Technology

AI Can See the Pattern. Can It See the Person?

For leaders, teams & technology organizations When machines can recognize the pattern, humans still have to understand what it means.

AI is changing how organizations build, decide and compete. Shaun explores why domain expertise, context, empathy and judgment don't become less important as AI gets better — they become the differentiator. The goal isn't choosing humans or AI. It's knowing what each should do.

03
Keynote · Nonprofit / Youth Development

Tech or Trap?

For nonprofits & youth organizations Technology should expand human capacity — not replace human connection.

Youth organizations are under constant pressure to adopt new technology. Shaun provides a practical framework for deciding what genuinely increases impact, what simply adds complexity, and how organizations can use technology while keeping young people, relationships and community at the center.
