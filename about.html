About Shaun

Photo: Shaun Jayachandran

Shaun Jayachandran bridges the worlds of cutting-edge technology and transformative social impact. With 20+ years of elite coaching experience developing 80+ college athletes, 8+ years building AI-powered products as a Senior Product Manager, and 12+ years founding and leading Hoops Creating Hope, he brings rare authenticity to conversations about scaling impact.

His speaking draws from real experience—not theory. He's helped scale products from $0–$5M+ ARR, mentored thousands of young people toward educational advancement, and navigated the complex intersection of technology innovation and social responsibility. A 2x TEDx speaker and M.Ed. in Educational Leadership, Shaun speaks to leaders, educators, parents, and organizations committed to using their platform purposefully.

Based in Maryland with his family, Shaun is available for keynotes, workshops, and speaking engagements throughout the year.
